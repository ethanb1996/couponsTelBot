# Repo Rules

- Do not propose or implement captcha bypassing, anti-bot evasion, or terms-of-service circumvention.
- Prefer MVP-first architecture over premature microservices.
- Prefer reversible decisions.
- Explicitly document assumptions, risks, and tradeoffs.
- Every major product decision should appear in a PRD or decision log.
- Every major architecture decision should appear in an ADR or architecture doc.
- Optimize for learning velocity, legal resilience, and operational simplicity.
